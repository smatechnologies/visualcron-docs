## My Settings Do Not Seem to Work

### Symptom

Changes that you made to a Job do not seem to take effect.

### Cause

A Job can have several Triggers, Tasks, Conditions and Notifications. The Job is scheduled with your changes only after each settings window that you changed is closed with *OK*.

### Fix

To apply your changes, complete the following steps:

1. In each settings window that you changed, click *OK*.
2. Check that the Server is on and the Job is active. See [Job Does Not Run](job-does-not-run).

   The Job runs with the new settings.
