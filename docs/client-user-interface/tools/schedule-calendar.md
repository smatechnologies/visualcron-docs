## Schedule - Calendar

The ***Calendar*** window shows when your Jobs are scheduled to run, so that you can, for example, spread Jobs that should not run at the same time.

To open it, click ***Tools*** -> ***Schedule*** -> ***Calendar***. Opening it requires the Read permission for Jobs; see [User Permissions](../server/main-user-permissions).

![Calendar window in Day view with the Jobs list on the left and scheduled runs on the right](../../../static/img/Client%20User%20Interface/Main%20Menu/Tools/Calendar.png)

### View

Switch between the ***Day***, ***Week*** and ***Month*** views. ***Today*** on the toolbar returns the view to the current date, and the calendar at the left moves it to another date. A slider in the status bar adjusts the detail of the view.

### Time exceptions

***Show Time exceptions*** shows the time exceptions in the calendar. ***Use Time exceptions*** takes them into account in the schedule.

### Jobs

The Jobs are listed at the left under _Filter Jobs_. Check or clear a Job to show or hide it in the calendar, and use ***Select all Jobs*** or ***Deselect all Jobs*** to change all of them at once. Right-click a Job to ***Open Job*** or ***Hide Job***. Point to a scheduled item to see its name, start and end time and duration.

### Print

***Print preview***, ***Page setup*** and ***Select printer*** on the toolbar print the calendar.
